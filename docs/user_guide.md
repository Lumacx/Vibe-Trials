# Vibe Retro Game Jam - User Guide

Welcome to the Vibe Retro Game Jam! This project features a collection of five fun, retro-inspired mini-games. This guide will help you get started.

## Navigating the Homepage

When you first open the game, you will land on the homepage. This is your central hub for accessing all the games. You will see a mystical environment with five distinct elemental symbols arranged in a star shape.

*   Each symbol represents a different game:
    *   A fiery icon for the **Fire** game.
    *   A stone icon for the **Stone** game.
    *   A swirling wind icon for the **Wind** game.
    *   A water ripple icon for the **Water** game.
    *   A leaf or tree icon for the **Nature** game.

To select a game, simply **click** on the corresponding elemental symbol.

In the center of the star, you may see a treasure chest. This might lead to a "Game Jam Showcase" or "Credits" section.

## Playing the Games

Each game has its own unique gameplay style, but there are some common elements you'll find:

*   **Timer:** Every game has a **90-second countdown timer**. The game will end when the timer reaches zero.
*   **Score:** Your performance in each game is tracked by a **score**. Earn points by achieving the game's objectives. Try to get the highest score you can before time runs out!
*   **Health:** Some games, like the Fire and Water games, will have a **health bar** or display. This shows how much damage your character can take before the game ends. Avoid obstacles and enemies to keep your health up!

**Common Controls:** While controls vary slightly by game, you will primarily use your **keyboard arrow keys** for movement and potentially the **Spacebar** or **Z key** for actions like shooting or using items. On mobile, on-screen controls may be provided.

### How to Play Each Game

Here's a quick guide on how to play each of the five games:

#### Flame Frenzy (Fire Game)

*   **Objective:** Cut as many Gems as you can with your flame sword to earn points.
*   **Controls:** Use your **mouse** or **touch** to drag your "flame sword" across the screen.
*   **Gameplay:** Objects (Gems, Ice Cubes, and Explosives) will fly up from the bottom. Slice the **Gems** to get points. Avoid cutting **Ice Cubes**, which will freeze your sword temporarily, and **Explosives**, which will reduce your health.
*   **Goal:** Get the highest score before the timer runs out or your health reaches zero.

#### Stone Labyrinth (Stone Game)

*   **Objective:** Navigate a maze from the start to the exit.
*   **Controls:** Use the **arrow keys** or an on-screen D-pad to move your character one space at a time (Up, Down, Left, Right).
*   **Gameplay:** You are in a labyrinth made of stone. Your goal is to reach the exit on the other side.
*   **Obstacles:** Watch out for **holes** in the ground! If you step into a hole, the game ends.
*   **Goal:** Reach the exit within the time limit.

#### Sky Guardian (Wind Game)

*   **Objective:** Shoot down incoming alien enemies using your wind whirl attack.
*   **Controls:** Use the **Left and Right arrow keys** or A/D to move your ship horizontally. Press **Spacebar** or **Z** to fire your wind whirl attack.
*   **Gameplay:** Control a wind-powered spacecraft at the bottom of the screen. Waves of wind aliens will descend from the top.
*   **Enemies:** Shoot the **wind aliens** before they reach you.
*   **Obstacles:** Avoid the projectiles fired by the aliens! Getting hit will end the game.
*   **Goal:** Survive as long as possible and get the highest score by destroying aliens within the time limit.

#### Hydro Heroes (Water Game)

*   **Objective:** Put out fires in a burning town maze and defeat flame monsters.
*   **Controls:** Use the **arrow keys** or an on-screen D-pad to move your firefighter character. Press **Spacebar** or **Z** to spray water from your hose.
*   **Gameplay:** Navigate through a town maze that's on fire. Use your water hose to extinguish burning houses and defeat flame monsters.
*   **Enemies:** **Flame monsters** will try to burn more houses and will damage you if they touch you.
*   **Goal:** Extinguish all fires (if applicable) and survive the flame monsters within the time limit. Your health bar shows how much damage you can take.

#### Forest Crossing (Nature Game)

*   **Objective:** Cross a busy highway to reach the forest on the other side.
*   **Controls:** Use the **arrow keys** or an on-screen D-pad to move your character one space at a time (Up, Down, Left, Right).
*   **Gameplay:** Guide your character (like a frog or woodland creature) across multiple lanes of traffic. You can only move one square at a time.
*   **Obstacles:** Avoid being hit by **cars and motorcycles** moving across the lanes. If a vehicle hits you, the game ends.
*   **Goal:** Reach the forest at the top of the screen within the time limit.

Remember to keep an eye on the timer and your score in all games!

## Game Over

When the timer runs out or you meet a loss condition (like losing all your health), the game will end. You will see a **Game Over screen** displaying your final score. From here, you can usually choose to retry the game or return to the homepage.

## Leaderboard

The game features a **leaderboard** to track high scores. After playing a game, your score may be recorded. You can view the leaderboard to see how your performance compares to others.

Have fun playing the Vibe Retro Game Jam!
